RBI $10 billion FX swap sails through on arbitrage demand

Reuters · 27 Sep 2026

Reuters reported Tuesday that the Reserve Bank of India's $10 billion dollar-rupee swap auction was oversubscribed as banks and corporates arbitraged the forward premium

The RBI offered a two-year sell/buy swap at a premium of 4.5% annualized. The onshore forward market quoted 5.2%. Banks borrowed dollars offshore at 5.5%, swapped with RBI at 4.5%, and lent rupees onshore at 7%. The arbitrage yielded 1.5% risk-free after hedging.

  • Reuters reported RBI's $10 billion FX swap auction was oversubscribed.
  • The swap offered a 4.5% annualized premium for two years.
  • Onshore forward market quoted a 5.2% premium.
  • Banks arbitraged the 0.7% gap between swap and forward rates.
  • Corporate demand for dollar hedging added to subscription.

Colophon & Method

Lens & Facts — how this paper chooses and frames

Selection, questions and framing are governed by an energy-literate scientific materialism and a dialectical method — facts are not. Every factual claim carries a source; the lens never alters a number, date or quote; a claim that contradicts the lens is reported, not suppressed. Settled evidence is called settled with citations; genuinely contested positions both appear with their evidence. News summaries, analysis and opinion are visually distinct, and analysis always reads through the lens. The machine audits itself in public: errors are corrected openly, and periodic "stories that broke our frame" exercises use the lens's own tools to test its default narrative. Funding, cost and conflicts are disclosed here, in the State of the Machine.

Coverage spans the region and the world. Opportunities are judged by a simple test: can a working person actually act on this, and does it improve their real conditions or their community's? Anything that gates its benefit behind money, status or existing wealth is excluded.
