Comparing Congress's Two AI Emergency Shutdown Mechanisms

LessWrong · 13th Aug · https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/nrcicbtdegbdmbuge/comparing-congress-s-two-ai-emergency-shutdown-mechanisms
  • The FRONTIER Act allows the Secretary of Commerce to issue emergency orders to suspend deployment, internal use, or training of frontier AI models when there is "imminent catastrophic risk," with penalties of $10 million/day and up to 10 years imprisonment for willful violations.
  • The AI Kill Switch Act grants the Secretary of Homeland Authority to issue orders through CISA after a "covered incident" occurs, suspending deployment and possibly internal use but not training, with penalties of $20 million/day and no specified time limit for orders.
  • The FRONTIER Act requires companies to maintain technical shutdown ability and verify compliance, while the AI Kill Switch Act does not explicitly require these measures and is not exclusive for suspending developers on imminent catastrophic risk grounds.
Why it matters: This comparison reveals critical differences in U.S. legislative approaches to AI emergency powers, highlighting trade-offs between speed, scope, and enforceability in frontier model regulation.
