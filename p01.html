Colophon & Method

Lens & Facts — how this paper chooses and frames

Selection, questions and framing are governed by an energy-literate scientific materialism and a dialectical method — facts are not. Every factual claim carries a source; the lens never alters a number, date or quote; a claim that contradicts the lens is reported, not suppressed. Settled evidence is called settled with citations; genuinely contested positions both appear with their evidence. News summaries, analysis and opinion are visually distinct, and analysis always reads through the lens. The machine audits itself in public: errors are corrected openly, and periodic "stories that broke our frame" exercises use the lens's own tools to test its default narrative. Funding, cost and conflicts are disclosed here, in the State of the Machine.

Coverage spans the region and the world. Opportunities are judged by a simple test: can a working person actually act on this, and does it improve their real conditions or their community's? Anything that gates its benefit behind money, status or existing wealth is excluded. Verification: no claim is marked fully verified without independent sources; verdicts are a prompt to read critically, not a substitute for it.
